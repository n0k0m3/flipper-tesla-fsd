Connect

  1. Stay near the board. The car must be awake.
  2. Tap Connect and select SCRL-xxxx.
  3. At the first connection, the phone asks for a passkey. Type the 6 digits from the label on the board.

The board accepts a new phone only in the first 5 minutes after power-on, or for 2 minutes after a press of its button. It drops a phone that does not finish the pairing in 30 seconds.

Use Chrome or Edge (Android, desktop), or Bluefy (iPhone). The phone asks for the passkey, not this page. The page handles a passkey only when you change it, and never stores it.

Change the passkey

The passkey is the 6-digit number that a phone needs for a new pairing. The phones that are paired stay paired: use Forget bonds to remove them. Write the new number on the label of the board before you save it.
